# 🚀 Feedora AI

**Turn Customer Feedback into Business Growth.**

Feedora AI is an AI-powered customer feedback intelligence platform designed to help small and local businesses make smarter, data-driven decisions.

It analyzes customer reviews using Google Gemini AI to identify sentiment, recurring complaints, customer preferences, and emerging demands. It then transforms these insights into practical business recommendations and marketing content.

### ✨ Key Features
- **Review Intelligence:** Analyze customer reviews and understand customer sentiment.
- **Demand Radar:** Identify recurring customer needs and emerging market demands.
- **AI Business Advisor:** Generate actionable recommendations to improve business performance.
- **AI Marketing Studio:** Create engaging marketing content for platforms like Instagram and WhatsApp.
- **Growth Tracker:** Monitor review trends and track business improvement over time.
- **Multi-Industry Support:** Demo datasets for restaurants, salons, and clothing businesses.

### 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Frontend:** React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Recharts
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js
- **Database:** MongoDB, Mongoose
- **AI Integration:** Google Gemini API
- **Data Processing:** PapaParse, Zod

### 🎯 Our Goal
To empower local businesses with AI-driven insights that help them understand their customers, improve their services, discover new opportunities, and grow smarter.
